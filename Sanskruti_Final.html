Chronicles of Time

Key epochs that shaped the sub-continent's legacy

2500 BCE – 1500 BCE

Indus Valley Civilization

One of the world's earliest urban civilizations known for sophisticated town planning, metallurgy, and trade.

322 BCE – 185 BCE

Maurya Dynasty

The rise of Emperor Ashoka, spreading Buddhism across Asia and establishing iconic rock and pillar edicts.

319 CE – 543 CE

The Golden Age (Guptas)

A flourishing era of classical art, mathematics (discovery of zero), astronomy, and literature.

1526 CE – 1857 CE

Mughal & Regional Architecture

An era of grandeur giving rise to world heritage marvels like the Taj Mahal, Red Fort, and Humayun's Tomb.
